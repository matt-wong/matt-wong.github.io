Roll or Die!!

In 2021 I released this game on the Android Google Play platform. It was built in Unity and predates AI vibe-coded games. For this game I wanted to complete something achievable, so I opted for an "Endless Runner" game with less focus on pure content and more on detail-oriented difficulty scaling.
